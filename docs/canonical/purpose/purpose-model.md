# Purpose and success model
## Overall human purpose
Help responsible maintainers, reviewers and sponsors reach trustworthy, comprehensible and appropriately authorized change decisions across differing project contexts, without unnecessary repeated coordination or concealed risk.
## Distinct purpose obligations
P01: trustworthy current change state. P02: informed bounded sponsor authorization. P03: honest recovery from unavailable review. P04: preservation of the owning project's local decision and security boundaries, independent of coordination mechanism. P05: independently challengeable findings. P06: accurate handoff and completion status. Broad protection against avoidable harm is a product-level purpose and evaluation guardrail, not a seventh concept-discriminating obligation.
See [001-C purpose decomposition and counterexamples](../../phases/001/001-c-purposes.md) for the distinct purpose rationale. Contributors and evidence subjects are affected parties whose needs remain hypotheses. P01 addresses evidence applicability, P05 finding challengeability, and P06 truthful completion status; their separability needs independent scrutiny.
## Evaluation posture
See [001-E](../../phases/001/001-e-success.md) for six representative success/failure situations. These are candidate tests, not validated quantitative thresholds.
## Stakeholders and tensions
See [001-B](../../phases/001/001-b-needs.md) and [001-D](../../phases/001/001-d-context-tensions.md). Sponsor assertions are not independent empirical validation. Operator population, baseline burden, costs, downstream impacts and project-specific requirements remain unresolved.
## Exclusions
No concept catalog, workflow schema, agent/provider selection, runtime, architecture or implementation choice is authorized by these purposes. Phase 001 exit is not independently accepted.
