## Divergent alternatives rather than a preferred decomposition
- **Evidence-centered possibility:** B01/B02/B08 anchor recognizable applicability and challenge behavior; B03/B09 remain separate sponsor authority; B06 captures delivery truth.
- **Case-centered possibility:** B07 organizes evidence, findings, decision and outcome as one user mental model; test whether it becomes a mere container or hides separate authorities.
- **Decision-centered possibility:** B03/B09/B12 organize protected actions and reconsideration, with evaluation and progress remaining independent.
- **Independent familiar constructs possibility:** Treat objections, permissions, pending evaluations and work outcomes as separate familiar mental models, linked only when user purposes demand it.
These are *contrasting discovery prompts*, not the structured decomposition comparison or selection reserved for 002-C/002-E.
