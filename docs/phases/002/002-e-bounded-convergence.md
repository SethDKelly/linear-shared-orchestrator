# 002-E — Bounded convergence

**Status: INDEPENDENT SCREENING PASS at e54c999 (2026-10-22) — provisional hypotheses only; no canonical promotion, final selection or implementation authorization.**

## Method and limit
The [002-B seeds](002-b-purpose-first-seeds.md), [002-C alternatives](002-c-alternative-decompositions.md) and [002-D adversarial challenges](002-d-cross-context-challenge.md) have independent divergent handoff PASS findings, not empirical user validation. Screen hypotheses for purpose, recognizable user-facing behavior, semantic distinctness, end-to-end meaning, plausible independence, familiarity and reusability. A useful cross-cutting protection is not automatically a standalone concept. **Retain** means provisionally worthy of Phase 003 behavioral challenge, not chosen product architecture or validated concept. No numerical ranking, quota, service or provider choice.

## Convergence challenge findings and return path
- **E-001 — Incumbent resemblance:** C1 lacks an established independent user-observable difference from S-81/PR #1. **Disposition:** retain as control alternative only, not a distinct concept; reopen 002-C if independent evidence supports another boundary.
- **E-002 — Authority separation:** C2/C6 risk conflating review, explanation and sponsor approval. **Disposition:** explicit disqualifier for Phase 003, no inferred permission; reopen 002-B/C if the separation proves impossible.
- **E-003 — Evidence subjects and local autonomy:** privacy/retention and project identity are not validated. **Disposition:** preserve B11/C5 constraints and hand off to adversarial Phase 003 specification; reopen 002-D if new affected-party evidence changes candidate boundaries.
- **E-004 — Unverified recovery utility:** C4 may be an implementation workflow rather than a user-facing concept. **Disposition:** test only as bounded interruption lens; reopen 002-C if a distinct end-to-end user experience is shown.

No finding presently establishes a **missing viable alternative** or uncovered provisional purpose that forces immediate re-divergence. These are explicitly recorded conditional triggers, not silent closure. A future material finding reopens the appropriate B/C/D record, documents the comparison and reruns E before F may close.

## Phase 003 handoff questions, not specifications
- What recognizable end-to-end action makes a living inquiry more than a change container?
- Can a permission/obligation mental model preserve sponsor authority and reviewer independence across local projects?
- How can a narrative remain comprehensible without laundering stale, missing or confidential evidence?
- Which actions, actors, scope and change events invalidate a prior review or authorization?
- How do pending, blocked, stopped, disputed and complete differ to affected users?
- What empirical interviews, accessibility studies, privacy/access/retention decisions, provider independence tests, cross-project validations and cost baselines are required before claiming fit?
