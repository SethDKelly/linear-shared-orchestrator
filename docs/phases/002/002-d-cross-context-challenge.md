## Familiar analogues, without solution import
- **Casework/inquiry:** ongoing claims, objections and revision history; challenge whether the case is merely a container.
- **Permission/consent:** explicit scope, revocation and actor authority; do not substitute review PASS for consent.
- **Audit trail:** reconstructable assertions and challenges; beware logging as a substitute for comprehensibility.
- **Incident handoff:** honest interruption, ownership and recovery; avoid presupposing an automated workflow engine.
- **Federated governance:** locally owned rules and mutual coordination; avoid assuming a central authority.
- **Narrative explanation:** user-facing clarity; do not allow explanation to launder stale or unverifiable evidence.
These analogues are prompts, not proof of fit or design choices.
