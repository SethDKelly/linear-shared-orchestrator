**Status: corrective REVIEW CANDIDATE, not independently qualified, not 005-D admission or accepted synchronization.** The accepted [005-C triage](005-c-obligation-seam-qualification.md) remains historical evidence; this record corrects the later-discovered composition-method gap under [S-98 sponsor clarification](https://linear.app/s-kelly/issue/S-98). ## Source-first method and decision boundary

Phase 005 **must** determine concept independence, application interdependence and **how and to what** any necessary synchronization applies. For each seam, inspect source purpose, action inputs/outputs, actor, identity, scope, revision, preconditions, authority, history and falsifying positive/negative paper traces. A missing pre-existing mapping is a Phase 005 design question, not a sponsor HOLD. Classify (a) qualified composition with source-valid bindings and guards, (b) independent/no **mandatory** synchronization with an explicit over-coupling counterexample, (c) intrinsic source defect and return owner/trigger, or (d) precise unresolved application policy/purpose conflict. Do not convert an optional *possible* linkage into a required one. This bounded CO-1–CO-4 assessment does **not** certify the entire application's composition complete or decide all later application actions.

## Source-supported CO-1–CO-4 dispositions and falsifiable traces

**CO-1 — C2 inquiry ↔ R-06 assessment/outcome: INDEPENDENT; NO MANDATORY SYNCHRONIZATION for current sources.** C2 [OpenInquiry/OfferGround/ChallengeGround/ProposeAnswer/InspectInquiry](../../canonical/concepts/c2-living-inquiry.md) maintains revision-qualified grounds and provisional answers; R-06 [RecordAssessment/AssertOutcome/InspectOutcome](../../canonical/concepts/r06-work-outcome-accountability.md) records work-unit/revision-attributed assessments, outcome assertions and their separate substantiation. C2 has no work-attempt identity; R-06 has no native inquiry identity. Intake's exact-source and explainable-decision promise can be fulfilled by independent inspection of each history; neither source requires every R-06 assessment to consume a C2 answer. **Positive trace:** an eligible actor records an R-06 assessment of an existing work unit/revision, while an unrelated C2 inquiry is opened, grounded and inspected; both retain their own claims and revisions without a cross-action trigger. **Negative/overcoupling trace:** a C2 answer on another work unit or stale revision is automatically fed into R-06 `RecordAssessment` or `AssertOutcome`, or a contested C2 ground is treated as substantiated completion—reject. A future *particular* evidence-citation composition would require an independently evidenced same-subject identity/revision binding, current applicable grounds, evidence credibility and a non-completion-proof guard; **none is qualified here**. No universal inquiry-to-work-unit mapping or synchronization edge admitted.

**CO-2 — C3 scoped permission ↔ R-06 work request: INDEPENDENT; NO MANDATORY REQUEST/PERMISSION SYNCHRONIZATION.** C3 [Authorize/InspectPermission](../../canonical/concepts/c3-scoped-authorization.md) records explicit actor-attributed project-local scope permission under its own authority predicate; R-06 [RequestWork](../../canonical/concepts/r06-work-outcome-accountability.md) records a bounded work request, not execution or permission. The current sources name no application `permitted-work` action, scope↔unit/revision mapping, or universal request guard. **Positive trace:** R-06 `RequestWork` records a request with no C3 grant, and C3 `InspectPermission` independently reports no current authorization; no execution is thereby permitted. Separately, a qualified actor can `Authorize` an exact scope without creating an R-06 work request. **Negative/overcoupling trace:** a work request, C2 `ProposeAnswer`, R-06 `RecordAssessment`/`AssertOutcome`/substantiated completion, favorable review PASS, or an external obligation assertion automatically creates C3 permission—reject. A future application action that actually **executes** work may require a scope↔unit/revision binding and a current C3 permission guard, but no such action/binding is qualified by these sources. Never confuse permission to execute with the ability to request.

**CO-3 — C2 rebase, C3 withdrawal, R-06 re-identification/invalidation: REJECT universal lifecycle synchronization; INDEPENDENT for unbound subjects.** [RebaseInquiry](../../canonical/concepts/c2-living-inquiry.md), [Withdraw](../../canonical/concepts/c3-scoped-authorization.md) and [ReidentifyWork/InvalidateBasis](../../canonical/concepts/r06-work-outcome-accountability.md) preserve independent histories and revoke only their own current applicability. Intake and the accepted concepts establish no common subject or requirement to cascade every change. **Positive trace:** R-06 re-identifies one work unit; its prior bases are historical, while unrelated C2 inquiry grounds and C3 scope decisions remain unchanged. **Negative/overcoupling trace:** that re-identification automatically withdraws unrelated C3 authorization or rebases an unrelated C2 inquiry—reject. A specific shared-subject visibility or applicability relation is **not qualified** without a source-grounded identity/revision link and a demonstrated application obligation; do not erase any historical record.

**CO-4 — C2 ground challenge ↔ R-06 outcome challenge: REJECT automatic pairing; INDEPENDENT challenge histories.** C2 [ChallengeGround/InspectInquiry](../../canonical/concepts/c2-living-inquiry.md) addresses an inquiry ground; R-06 [ChallengeOutcome/InspectOutcome](../../canonical/concepts/r06-work-outcome-accountability.md) addresses an outcome assertion and material difference. No canonical mapping equates a ground with an outcome assertion. **Positive trace:** an inquiry ground is challenged and remains visible in C2; R-06 inspection independently shows its outcome assertion's actual contest/substantiation state, without inventing a second challenge. **Negative/overcoupling trace:** a C2 ground objection automatically becomes a material R-06 contest, a `NOT SUBSTANTIATED` objection is erased or treated as proof, or `CONTESTED — SAFETY REVIEW PENDING` is reported as PASS or silently restored to current substantiation—reject. A narrower combined *awareness* view requires a particular ground↔assertion binding and qualified RD-15 policy; neither is supplied here. Do not invent a mandatory R-06 adjudication/challenge-resolution action.

**Result for these four seams:** the current accepted purposes/actions do **not** establish a mandatory cross-concept synchronization edge. This is a positive, source-limited **no-mandatory-sync classification**, not a claim that concepts never interact or that Phase 005 synchronization analysis is complete. 005-D, if separately admitted, must determine deliberate application action exposure and any further purpose-supported bindings, triggers, effects, independence and negative traces; it cannot treat speculative mappings as already accepted. An independently evidenced new application promise can reopen only the affected seam with its owner and falsifier, not all four by default.
