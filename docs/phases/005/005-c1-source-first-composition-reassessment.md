## Required method and classification

Phase 005 is obligated to determine **which concepts remain independent, where application behavior makes them interdependent, and how any necessary synchronization constrains participating actions**. A missing *pre-existing* cross-concept mapping is a design question for this phase, not itself a source-owner HOLD. Use the [composition contract](composition-synchronization-contract.md) and source-owned concept specifications to examine: purpose and observable application behavior; participants and their preconditions; shared identity/scope/revision applicability; conceptual trigger and binding; authority; effects/history; a positive trace; a negative counterexample; and over-/under-synchronization. Classify **qualified composition**, **independence/no synchronization**, **source defect with return trigger**, or **precise unresolved policy/purpose conflict**. A proposed coupling may be rejected without rejecting the obligation to analyze the application's complete composition.
