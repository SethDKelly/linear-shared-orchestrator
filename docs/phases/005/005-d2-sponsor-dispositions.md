## Scope and decision authority

- **Scope proposal and clarification:** TPO, concept source owner, and implementer may each propose bounded scope and collaboratively resolve ambiguity. A proposal or technical review is not permission.
- **Project-local authorized actor:** a **human decision maker**; for this project the sponsor explicitly identifies themself as that actor. This is an accountable project-level decision role, not a blanket grant to an agent, implementer, reviewer, or other person. For any C3 permission decision, exact actor, scope and basis remain required; a materially different scope cannot inherit a grant.
- **TPO:** routes scope qualification, actor/source-owner evidence, dependencies, review and acceptance; TPO does not manufacture source-owner confirmation. A missing Linear assignee is metadata debt.
- **Independent reviewer:** tests the existing contract, source fidelity, negative traces and materiality; review PASS cannot itself create C3 permission.
