## Negative and boundary obligations for any later qualified seam

1. **Authority:** C3 `Authorize` requires its own explicit actor-attributed, project-local, scope-guarded decision. C2 answers, R-06 claims, review PASS and obligation assertions cannot supply permission by themselves.
2. **Truth and safety:** R-06 request, attempt, assessment, assertion and current substantiation remain distinct. Preserve `NOT SUBSTANTIATED`, material `CONTESTED`, independent credible-suspicion investigation, and `CONTESTED — SAFETY REVIEW PENDING` suspension of affected *current* substantiation. No mandatory challenge-resolution action.
3. **Revision and history:** C2 `RebaseInquiry` and R-06 `ReidentifyWork` invalidate presumed current applicability without erasing historical claims. C3 `Withdraw` preserves permission history without current grant. A cross-concept link must explicitly justify identity and revision applicability.
4. **Source defect return:** Missing intrinsic action semantics return to qualified Phase 003/004 owners; a source-complete RD-14 generic-case collapse returns first to Phase 002-C. CF-1 `InspectInquiry` inspection completeness must be resolved by its source owner **before** direct exposure or 005-D use, not by inventing a Phase 005 workaround. RD-15 remains qualified-policy-owner work. Q4/Q5 stay frozen absent materially distinct intrinsic evidence.
5. **Scope:** No accepted synchronization trigger, participating-action binding, application-action exposure, automation, chaining or synergy is asserted here. Phase 006 product inclusion, Phase 007 representation, Phase 008 reuse, implementation, AWS, security S-40 HOLD and releases remain separate.
