# 001-B — Affected-party needs and evidence
Status: ANALYSIS COMPLETE; independent exit review pending.
| Party | Burden or need | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | Understand what was reviewed and what remains blocked before authorizing protected actions | Direct stakeholder history; not independent measurement |
| Maintainer | Avoid repeating review/status reconciliation and losing cumulative findings | TreatFlow/aws-backbone historical cases; current rates unmeasured |
| Delivery agent | Receive bounded, unambiguous work and know when to stop | Hypothesis from historical handoff friction |
| Independent reviewer | Access stable source, context and disposition without self-certification | Existing review cycle behavior; effectiveness unmeasured |
| Provider operator | Surface unavailability without it being confused with approval | Stakeholder-reported Cursor/Codex quota incidents; not independently reverified |
| Contributor | Know whether a request, review or change has actually completed | Hypothesis based on historical handoffs; no contributor interviews |
| Evidence subject / sensitive-data owner | Avoid unnecessary exposure of credentials, identities or confidential evidence | Derived privacy/security hypothesis; no direct interviews |
| Downstream user | Be protected from incorrect changes, delayed fixes and invisible risk | Derived affected-party hypothesis; direct interviews absent |
Needs not established: organization-wide scale, exact user populations, operational SLOs, regulatory requirements, cost thresholds. No actor's preference automatically overrides repository security authority.
