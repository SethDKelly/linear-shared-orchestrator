# 000-E — Boundaries, constraints and vocabulary
Status: COMPLETED provisionally.
## Scope
Contemplated capability: cross-repository review and bounded agentic delivery coordination, including evidence provenance, decision traceability, provider availability, finding reconciliation and repository policy isolation.
## Non-goals
Replacing GitHub source control, replacing Linear issue ownership, assuming project-specific merge/release/security-exit authority, privileged infrastructure execution, silently migrating TreatFlow, and embedding incomplete orchestrator or Linear onboarding patterns back into Base now.
## Vocabulary (historical working terms; not approved concept semantics)
Review: bounded independent evaluation. Finding: concern requiring disposition. Receipt: evidence of an observation, not approval. Exact-head: review tied to a particular commit. HOLD: cannot advance on current evidence. Shadow mode: read-only comparison without enforcement. Security exit: separately authorized project-specific acceptance. Sponsor authorization: explicit human approval for specified sensitive action.
## External boundaries
Project policy cannot be weakened by shared defaults; credentials and evidence must be repository-scoped; stale/missing/contradictory evidence cannot become PASS. Exact platform and regulatory constraints remain to be researched.
